---
title: Privacy
description: What vBase and vBase Imager send, and where.
---

- **vBase** runs on your servers and keeps your data there. It sends nothing to ONECOMET.
- **vBase Imager** contacts GitHub only: the release list when it starts, and the release you choose when you write a stick. No telemetry, no sign-in.
- **This site** is static pages on GitHub Pages, with no tracking.

Planned, and off unless an administrator turns it on: optional crash reports to ONECOMET (the error and versions only, never tenants' data), and problem reports that an administrator chooses to send.
